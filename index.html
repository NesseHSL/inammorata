The Founder

Twenty years of Italy.
Not touring it. Living inside it.

It started with wine. Years spent working in the Italian wine trade brought me back to Italy again and again, to its producers, its tables, its rhythms, until the country stopped feeling like somewhere I visited and started feeling like somewhere I belonged. I eventually got married in Florence, in the centre of the city I love most, because there was nowhere else I could imagine it happening. That relationship, specific, personal, built over decades, is what Innamo is made of.

I know which Florentine trattoria the locals actually eat in, and which ones they quietly avoid. I know the Sicilian masseria that looks extraordinary in photographs and delivers on every one of them. I know the Val d’Orcia in autumn, the Amalfi Coast before the season, and the parts of Puglia that don’t yet have a gift shop.

Not every recommendation comes from me personally. Italy is large, and I’m honest about that. The places I haven’t experienced myself are covered by a small circle of trusted local contacts I’ve spent years cultivating. What every recommendation shares is the same standard: it has to be worth your time.

Innamo exists because the Italy most people visit and the Italy worth visiting are not always the same place. Closing that gap is my job, and my genuine pleasure.

Nesse

Questions, Answered

What does Innamo do?

Innamo is a bespoke luxury travel company specialising exclusively in Italy. We plan personally curated Italian itineraries, book hand-picked hotels and villas with exclusive perks and preferred rates, and can take care of an entire trip: restaurants, transfers, private experiences and tickets.

Which parts of Italy do you cover?

All of Italy, including Florence and the Tuscan countryside, Siena, Rome, Venice, Verona, Lake Como, the Cinque Terre, Portofino, the Amalfi Coast and Capri, Sardinia, Sicily and Puglia.

How much does it cost to plan a trip with Innamo?

Accommodation bookings made through Innamo are always complimentary. A curation fee applies for itinerary planning and any non-accommodation arrangements, quoted per trip once we understand what you would like. We offer three levels of service: Il Percorso (a bespoke itinerary you book yourself), Il Soggiorno (the itinerary plus accommodation booked for you) and L’Esperienza (a fully managed Italian trip).

Who is behind Innamo?

Innamo was founded by Nesse, who has spent twenty years in and around Italy, much of it in the Italian wine trade, and married in Florence. Innamo is run by Innamo Travel Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales.

What kind of trips do you plan?

Honeymoons, anniversaries, milestone birthdays, wine and food trips, and any journey through Italy that needs to be just right. We also host an annual Wine & Wellness Retreat in Tuscany with HerSpace London.

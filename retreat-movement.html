The Rhythm of the Day

Wellness, in the fullest sense.

Mornings here start slowly, with the light. Depending on the day, you’ll wake to Pilates, barre, or a softer BEND-Y flow, either at sunrise or as the sun dips in the evening — sometimes both, if you’re up for it. Two sessions a day, led by our Founder and Head of Pilates, in small groups — indoors in the villa’s cool stone hall when the light hasn’t quite arrived yet, or outside under the shade tents and open sky once it has.

No Pressure, Just Movement

Every level welcome. Every session optional.

This isn’t about performance. Sessions are small, unhurried, and shaped around the group in the room — whatever your experience with Pilates, barre, or movement generally. Join every session, or none of them. Stretch out by the pool instead if that’s what the day calls for. This is your holiday, and it should feel exactly like the one you want.

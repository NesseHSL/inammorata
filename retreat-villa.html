The Villa

A restored medieval watchtower and its grounds, near Siena.

Villa Cecconi

Three Stone Buildings. A Century of Heritage.

Villa Cecconi is spread across three stone buildings — an original medieval watchtower forms the main guest villa, with a second, smaller villa providing the remaining accommodation. Each part of the property has been carefully restored, retaining its original exposed stone walls, terracotta tiles, and wooden beamed ceilings.

Grounds & Exterior

Room to spread out, wherever you land.

Beyond the walls, the grounds do their own quiet work. A long pool with its own wet bar, shaded by a canopy sail. A pergola-covered dining table, family-style, looking out over the hills. Manicured lawns dotted with olive and cypress. And when the sun gets too much, there’s always another covered seating area to retreat to — with a glass of something cold, obviously.

The Rooms

En suite. Queen beds. Yours for the week.

All rooms are en suite, and offered as single, private accommodation with queen-sized beds, though some can also be shared between two — given on a first-come, first-served basis, so do book soon if you’d like to share.

Bedrooms and bathrooms are cleaned daily, and fresh linens are provided twice throughout the week. Goodie bags and bathrobes are provided — yours to keep, and yours to wear anywhere on the property, any time you like.

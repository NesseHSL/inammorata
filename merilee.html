Celebrating ten years, in Tuscany

To mark Merilee's tenth anniversary, we're gathering in the Tuscan countryside just outside Siena. Below are the two villas we've chosen for the trip — have a look through the photos and details for each ahead of your stay.

Option One · Poggibonsi

The Wine Estate

The Estate

A private wine estate in the Tuscan countryside, offered for your group's exclusive use — every villa, garden and gathering space on the property is yours alone, with nothing shared. The estate is made up of five individual apartments, each with its own entrance and outdoor space, so everyone has a corner to call their own while still gathering easily for meals, wine and long afternoons by the pool.

Fully equipped kitchens throughout mean you can cook as much or as little as you like, and the outdoor spaces are made for the in-between moments — morning coffee, sunset aperitivo, dinner under the stars.

On the Property

A private pool, a barbecue area, and a traditional brick pizza oven for evenings that turn into something more festive. For quieter moments, there are bicycles, bocce, table tennis, and a hammock with views rolling out over the vineyards — the estate sits right in the middle of the Montalpruno vines, and harvest season here is something to see.

Location

Ten minutes from Poggibonsi, with its restaurants, markets and everyday Tuscan life. The Chianti Classico wine region is just beyond that, San Gimignano's medieval towers are twenty minutes away, and Siena and Florence are both under forty-five. Complimentary parking on site; Florence airport is under an hour's drive.

Option Two · Poggibonsi

The Manor House

The House

An authentic Tuscan manor house, furnished in the old style and entirely at your disposal — the kind of place where you feel the shift the moment you walk through the gate. Spread across two floors, it holds a grand entrance hall, a dining room built for long meals, a proper Tuscan kitchen with an open fireplace, and a bright, generous living room with a real football table for anyone who fancies it. Upstairs, four double bedrooms and three bathrooms.

The Garden

Hidden behind a thick green hedge: an equipped pool (5 x 12m), sun loungers, a gazebo for breakfast, and a separate table made for long outdoor dinners. Lavender and roses line the lawn, and there's a barbecue and table tennis for anyone restless after dinner. Free WiFi and laundry throughout, and covered parking just off the garden.

Location

Barely two kilometres from Poggibonsi, reachable on foot along a quiet riverside path in about ten minutes — restaurants, cafés, shops and the train station are all there waiting. San Gimignano is ten kilometres away, and the Florence–Siena motorway is minutes from the gate, putting all of Tuscany comfortably within reach.

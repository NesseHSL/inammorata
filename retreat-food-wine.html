What You’ll Taste

Food & Wine

Long villa dinners, a day in Siena, and two Chianti wine tastings.

The Menu

Healthy and decadent, in the same breath.

Our very own Charlotte Baldwin brings the healthy and the decadent together in a menu planned with Nesse (AfN registered Clinical Nutritionist, ANut, and self-certified food snob), which will include:

  • Daily continental breakfast, with fresh juices and healthy smoothies
  • Abundant salads at lunchtime, with fresh fruit and wine to compliment
  • Fresh pastas and other delights for dinner — plus wine, cheese, bubbles, and lots and lots of laughter

All meals served at the villa are vegetarian/pescatarian.

The Wine

Two tastings. Both unforgettable, for different reasons.

Twice during the week, we’ll head out to taste our way through the region with two separate wine tours and tastings — the first, at Antinori Toscana, one of the most famous wine estates in Italy, is included in your stay. The second, at Castello di Ama, is available as an optional add-on.

And on one full day, we’ll swap the villa for Siena, wandering the Campo and its cobbled backstreets with our own private guide, before settling in for an early dinner in the city — entirely your own time to explore. By the time we’re back at the villa, it’s onto a lighter wine and cheese “dinner,” the kind of evening that asks nothing more of you than good company.

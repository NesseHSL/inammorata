How Innamo works

Simple, transparent,
and always worth it.

Most people who enquire with Innamo have never worked with a travel advisor before. This page is for them.

The honest answer

Will this cost me more than booking myself?

Almost never. Usually the opposite. When Innamo books your hotel, the hotel pays us a commission. This is how the travel advisory industry has worked for decades. You pay exactly the rate you would pay if you booked directly, sometimes less, because of our relationships with specific properties. What you gain, through our Virtuoso membership, are benefits that direct bookers simply cannot access: room upgrades, complimentary breakfast, late checkout, and property credits. The value of these additions, across a trip, routinely exceeds any planning fee several times over.

Where Innamo does charge a fee, it covers Nesse’s time: the introductory call, the research, the itinerary, the recommendations, and everything she has learned from twenty years of visiting Italy. It is not a booking fee. It is a creative and advisory fee, for work that takes genuine expertise to do well.

Where it begins

A short, complimentary call. No commitment, no pressure.

Every Innamo journey starts the same way: a short introductory call with Nesse. Fifteen minutes, free, with no obligation on either side. It is a chance for Nesse to understand where you want to go, what kind of traveller you are, and what you are hoping to feel by the end of the trip. And for you to get a sense of whether Innamo is the right fit.

From there, Nesse will suggest the level of service that makes sense for what you have in mind, and talk through any fees that apply before anything is agreed. You will never receive a bill you were not expecting.

I

Il Percorso

The Route

A bespoke written itinerary, built around your specific trip. Where to eat, what to see, how to spend your time, and the things worth knowing that do not appear in guidebooks. Nesse’s personal recommendations, tailored to you.

A small planning fee applies, discussed at your introductory call.

II

Il Soggiorno

The Stay

Everything in Il Percorso, plus Innamo handles your hotel bookings directly. Hotel bookings are always complimentary: the property pays our commission, and you gain access to Virtuoso benefits unavailable to direct bookers.

If you already know where you want to stay and simply want the booking handled and the benefits unlocked, there is no fee at all. A planning fee applies only if you would like a full itinerary alongside the hotel booking.

Hotel bookings: always complimentary. Planning fee from £75, if required.

III

L’Esperienza

The Experience

The full service. Hotels, restaurants, transfers, private experiences, and a comprehensive itinerary, all arranged. You arrive, and everything is already taken care of.

A planning fee applies, reflecting the scope and complexity of the trip. Hotel commissions are already included in this model. There is no double charging.

Planning fees apply, discussed and agreed before anything is confirmed. Scope and fee depend on the complexity and duration of your trip.

What you are actually paying for

Innamo is not a booking engine with a service charge. It is a person who knows Italy with real intimacy, who has spent twenty years building the relationships and the knowledge that make a trip genuinely extraordinary. The planning fee, where it applies, is for that expertise and that time.

The hotel commission, paid by the property, is the industry’s way of saying thank you for the business. It has nothing to do with what you pay. And the Virtuoso benefits it unlocks: the upgrades, the early check-ins, the credits, the welcome amenities, are the part that most clients remember longest.
